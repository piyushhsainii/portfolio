# C06 / First-bottle invitation / 21 October 2026

Problem: the original $38 sample framing used internal labels as consumer copy, gave no introductory advantage and repeated dense serif typography. The user requested a more convincing offer, typography and copy.

Proposed fictional offer: one 30 mL bottle, $29 first order, free US shipping, no subscription; tax extra. No prior-price comparison or percentage saving is asserted. These are campaign design assumptions, not an active store offer or validated profitable terms. The earlier $38 offer is superseded.

Three executions: (1) graphic value-first poster; (2) tactile unboxing with purchase-control copy; (3) dark premium editorial first-bottle invitation. Modern sans headline hierarchy replaces compressed heavy serif; the serif wordmark stays on the approved pack. A small concept disclosure replaces mockup language in the sales headline. No hair-growth, shedding, finish or safety claim is introduced.

Offer economics must be rechecked: $29 net AOV − $9 illustrative COGS − $5 illustrative fulfillment/shipping − $2 illustrative fees/returns = $13 contribution before ads. A $4 retained target leaves $9 allowable first-order CAC; break-even paid ROAS is 2.23×. Free shipping is funded by the fulfillment assumption, not added a second time. Actual shipping costs, tax collection, margins and eligibility require verification. No conversion improvement is measured or promised.
