# Creative review / internal editorial assessment

Scope: 18 complete static executions across six concepts, three per concept. No campaign performance, Meta-account measurement or clinical review. User approved the visual packaging; internal review is not a separate human launch approval.

## Ten craft checks

1. Buyer relevance: each concept names a buyer question and research insight. Hypotheses, not verified segments.
2. First glance: headline is the largest communication; pack and next step remain identifiable.
3. Copy hierarchy: headline, support and next step are present in artwork. Exact specifications in data/ads.json.
4. Brand: amber dropper, cream label, cobalt identity remain recognizable; generated typography/material details can vary slightly.
5. Format diversity: diagram, collage, routine, planner/sculpture, evidence folders, annotation, newspaper, anatomy, unboxing, macro, question card, zine, conversation, value poster, tactile unboxing and dark editorial differ in dominant premise. Same pack and editorial typography are intentional shared cues.
6. Awareness: Unaware 3; Problem aware 3; Solution aware 6; Product aware 3; Most aware 3. Top 6 / Mid 9 / Bottom 3.
7. Credibility: no efficacy, sensory or treatment result invented. Category listening is not testimonial proof. Future real proof concepts are parked.
8. Offer integrity: $29/30 mL first order with free US shipping and no subscription is a proposed fictional offer; tax extra. No reference-price saving, scarcity or outcome claim.
9. Placement: feed masters 1080×1350; Story exports 1080×1920 with complete art inset at x81/y310, size918×1148. No crop of original copy; adaptation retains large top/bottom margins. These are not bespoke motion/video assets.
10. Learning integrity: visual and copy packages change together. Ratings are editorial judgments, not measured attention or a Meta similarity score. No claim of guaranteed separate retrieval or lower costs.

## Deliberate limits

Some photographs share the cream/cobalt bathroom/editorial world, and most retain a prominent headline. The portfolio varies the dominant image and layout rather than promising maximum possible stylistic distance. Three executions per concept meet the user scope; the suite’s aspirational ten-plus and video volume are not claimed.

Actual product facts are needed before persuasive efficacy or sensory claims, instructions, expert content and real testimonials can be produced. A category education campaign is not validated demand for this product. A launch also requires actual terms, destination and event QA.

## Follow-up experiment

After sufficient valid observation, retain one scene/format/offer/persona and compare hook wording only. Reconcile purchases and contribution after reporting lag. Do not select winners from unequal exposure or sparse data. No arbitrary Andromeda threshold or fixed frequency fatigue rule is used.

C06 revision: the approved packaging is retained; modern sans display replaces the original heavy serif. Value, purchase control and first-bottle framing differ in image, layout and message. Conversion impact remains untested.
